Beyond Obvious Answers

Why Creative Thinking Matters

Creativity isn't only about being artistic.

It helps children ask:

💡

What else could this be?

🔍

Is there another way?

🧩

What happens if I combine these ideas?

🚀

How could I make this better?

Creative Spark gives children repeated opportunities to practise exactly that.

Curriculum Highlights

What Your Child Will Explore

Activities encourage children to:

●

generate multiple possibilities;

●

observe familiar things differently;

●

ask imaginative questions;

●

combine unrelated ideas;

●

transform existing ideas;

●

experiment without needing one “correct” answer;

●

improve an idea through reflection.

Playful Worksheets & Prompts

Inside Creative Spark

Expect activities such as:

Activity 01

Unusual Uses

How many different uses can you imagine for one object?

Activity 02

Mix Two Ideas

Combine unrelated concepts to create something new.

Activity 03

Change It

Take an existing product or idea and redesign one part.

Activity 04

Finish the Idea

Complete an unfinished concept in your own way.

Activity 05

Make It Better

Review an idea and identify how you could improve it.

The Mindset Shift

What They'll Walk Away With

Not a collection of perfect drawings.

A stronger belief that:

“I can come up with ideas-and I don't have to stop at the first one.”
