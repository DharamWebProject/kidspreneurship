Why These Skills Matter

Children manage more than we often realise:

Getting ready for school
Remembering homework
Keeping track of belongings
Completing a project
Managing distractions
Deciding what to do first

These everyday moments offer opportunities to build independence.

Everyday Application

Children can use the journal to think through situations such as:

“I have three things due tomorrow. What should I do first?”

“I keep forgetting my sports kit. What system could help?”

“My project feels too big. How can I break it down?”

The objective is not perfection.

It is helping children develop strategies they can increasingly use for themselves.
